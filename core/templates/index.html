Myth Busters

Separating fact from fiction about blood donation.

Myth: Donating blood makes you weak.

Fact: Your body replenishes the donated fluid within 24 hours and red blood cells within a few weeks. You can resume normal activities the next day.

Myth: It is a painful procedure.

Fact: You only feel a slight pinch when the needle goes in. The actual donation process is practically painless.

Myth: You can get an infection.

Fact: A new, sterile needle is used for every donor and discarded immediately. There is zero risk of contracting infections like HIV.

Myth: I can't donate if I have high blood pressure.

Fact: As long as your blood pressure is controlled by medication and within the acceptable range on donation day, you can safely donate.
